# Spec — RFQ follow-up contact (no WhatsApp tags)

## Marketplace submit

- When a **logged-in Mattex Marketplace member** submits an RFQ, copy their registration / account phone onto the RFQ (`buyerPhone`).
- **Guest** RFQs stay empty until Sales adds a number (guests have no marketplace account phone).

## Sales portal RFQ detail · Follow-up

- Sales **edit the buyer contact number** here (saved on the RFQ; also updates a member account phone).
- Member RFQ: show the marketplace account phone immediately. Copy: contact from the marketplace account; click the number to open WhatsApp.
- Member RFQ with no phone on file: Sales can add one.
- Guest RFQ: empty until Sales types a number.
- If the number can open WhatsApp (`wa.me`), **click the number** to jump to that chat. No “WhatsApp” pill / tag.
- Do **not** use a “WhatsApp buyer” button.

## Marketplace register / profile

- Remove the **“This number has WhatsApp” checkbox**.
- Do not show a WhatsApp tag next to the buyer phone.
